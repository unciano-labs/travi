# Traveler App Features
# Orgy SaaS - Traveler Account

This document outlines the user interface menus and corresponding features for the **Traveler** role.

---

## 1. Explore / Browse Tours
The primary discovery interface for travelers to find and book experiences.

- **Search & Filter:** Search by location, filter by price range, dates, and duration.
- **Tour Details:** View full tour information including title, description, price, capacity, and transport type.
- **Itinerary View:** See the day-by-day breakdown of the tour.
- **Booking Flow:** 
  - Select the number of seats to book.
  - See real-time validation for available capacity.
  - Proceed to checkout/payment.

## 2. My Bookings
The centralized hub for a traveler to manage their purchased tours.

- **Booking History:** List of all bookings organized by upcoming and past trips.
- **Booking Status:** Track the status of each booking (Pending, Confirmed, Canceled, Refunded, Completed).
- **Booking Details:** View payment summaries (total amount = price × seats), tour dates, and meeting points.
- **Actions:** Ability to cancel bookings (subject to policy) or view receipts.

## 3. Reviews
Where travelers can share their experiences.

- **Leave a Review:** Submit a rating (1-5 stars) and an optional comment for a completed tour or transport experience.
- **Past Reviews:** View previously submitted reviews.

## 4. Profile & Settings
Personal account management.

- **Personal Info:** Update full name, avatar, and contact details.
- **Account Security:** Update email and password.
