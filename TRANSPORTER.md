# Transporter App Features
# Orgy SaaS - Transporter Account

This document outlines the user interface menus and corresponding features for the **Transporter** role.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview
A high-level view of the transporter's fleet and operations.

- **Metrics & KPIs:** Total active vehicles, pending transport requests, upcoming trips.
- **Recent Activity:** Latest requests from Organizers and recently completed trips.

## 2. Vehicles Fleet
Manage the inventory of available vehicles.

- **Vehicle Roster:** List of all owned vehicles and their active/inactive status.
- **Add/Edit Vehicle:** 
  - Set vehicle details: name, type (car, van, bus, minibus, suv), and seat capacity.
  - Add optional details: license plate number, daily rate, description, and photos.
- **Deactivation:** Temporarily or permanently mark vehicles as inactive so they cannot be requested.

## 3. Availability Calendar
Control when vehicles can be booked.

- **Availability Management:** Set specific date ranges when a vehicle is available for booking.
- **Block Dates:** Manually block out dates for maintenance or offline bookings.
- **Conflict Prevention:** System visually prevents Organizers from requesting a vehicle on blocked or already-booked dates.

## 4. Transport Requests (Inbox)
Handle incoming business from Tour Organizers.

- **Request Inbox:** View all incoming transport requests (tour details, dates, pickup/dropoff, required seats).
- **Action Requests:** Accept or Decline pending requests.
- **Automatic Sync:** Accepting a request automatically generates a "booked" status in the Availability Calendar, blocking the vehicle for those dates.
- **Trip History:** View past and completed transport services.

## 5. Reviews
Monitor reputation and feedback.

- **Review Dashboard:** View all reviews received from Travelers or Organizers after completed trips.
- **Ratings:** See overall average rating and total review count.

## 6. Profile & Settings
Business profile management.

- **Business Info:** Manage transporter type (Individual or Company). Set company name, display name, and business description.
- **Service Areas:** Define and update the cities or regions where transport services are offered.
- **Contact Details:** Update phone number.
